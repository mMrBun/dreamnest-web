MOP TO-DO / SUPPORT

Help & support

Find answers about tasks, iCloud, reminders, widgets, backups, and purchases. If you are still stuck, email the developer below.

Can I use it without signing in?

Yes. No separate app account is required, and tasks are stored locally by default. Add, edit, organize, and complete tasks without iCloud. Map search and cloud sync need a network connection.

How do I sync across iPhone, iPad, and Mac?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device and enable iCloud sync in app settings. Fully quit and reopen the app after changing the switch. The system schedules sync, so updates may take time.

Why is iCloud not syncing?

Check that your devices use the same Apple Account, have a working connection and enough iCloud space, and have app sync enabled. Check the status or error in app settings and retry as directed. Export a backup before troubleshooting, then email the developer with the error. Avoid uninstalling the app as a troubleshooting step.

Why did a reminder not appear?

Enable reminders in the app and allow notifications in system settings. The task needs a scheduled time and a reminder that has not already passed. Focus modes, notification summaries, and system scheduling can affect presentation. Saved places are task information; they do not trigger arrival reminders.

How do I restore a task or make a backup?

Open Recently Deleted in settings to restore tasks that have not been permanently deleted. Import / Export includes folders, tasks, images, and recently deleted content. Imports are validated and previewed: add missing records by default, or choose to replace matching records. Store unencrypted backup files securely.

Why has my widget not updated yet?

The system schedules widget refreshes and some actions, so the display may lag. Open the main app, check its folder and sync status, and confirm the folder selected for the widget. On Mac, cloud delivery of changes made while the app is closed may continue when it next runs.

Are image or current-location permissions required?

No. Basic text tasks work without them. The system picker imports only images you select. Device location is requested when you choose to use your current location. Manage permissions in system settings.

Does buying a coffee renew automatically?

No. It is an optional, one-time Apple in-app purchase, with no subscription or additional task features. All task features remain available without tipping. Confirm the price in the App Store payment sheet. Check purchase status and refund options through Apple purchase history and support.

What should I do before changing devices or Apple Accounts?

Export a complete backup in settings and confirm you can access the file. Use the same Apple Account and sync settings on a new device. Turn sync off before changing accounts to avoid merging local content into another account. Turning sync off or uninstalling does not automatically delete cloud data.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iOS / iPadOS 16 or later and macOS 15 or later. The app offers Simplified Chinese and English, selectable in settings. Widget and appearance capabilities vary by system version.
