Mop To-Do Privacy Policy

English简体中文繁體中文日本語한국어

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Mop To-Do (拖把待办), developed by Hui Xu, helps you record, organize, and complete personal tasks. This policy describes information handling in the app and on this website.

1. Tasks and on-device storage

No separate app account is required. Folders, task titles, notes, completion and deletion states, dates, reminder settings, selected places, and image attachments are stored in the app database on your device. Language and appearance preferences are also stored locally. The developer does not operate an application server that receives this task content.

Widgets use shared app data on your device to display tasks and handle actions. Selected content may appear on your Home Screen, desktop, or other supported widget surfaces. Configure widgets to suit your privacy needs.

2. Optional iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default. When enabled, the app uses an Apple CloudKit private database to sync tasks, folders, image attachments, and related records under your Apple Account across your devices. It also checks connectivity with Apple's iCloud website without sending task content. Network connections reveal connection information such as your IP address to the service provider.

We do not receive your Apple Account password. Apple handles cloud data under its service terms; see Apple Account & Privacy. Sync depends on your network, account, and system scheduling and may not be immediate. Turning sync off does not automatically delete local or existing cloud data.

3. Images, places, and reminders

  • Images: the system photo or file picker imports only images you select. The app creates compressed attachment copies and syncs them if iCloud sync is enabled.
  • Places: map browsing, search, and place selection use Apple Maps. Search terms, map regions, and relevant location information may be sent to Apple. The app requests device location only when you choose to use your current location and grant permission. It does not continuously track your location in the background. Saved place names, addresses, and coordinates become part of your task. See Apple Maps & Privacy.
  • Reminders: with notification permission, time-based reminders are scheduled on your device. Notifications may show task titles and related content. Manage permissions and previews in system settings. Saved places do not trigger geofence reminders.

Basic text tasks remain available if you decline these permissions.

4. Backups and deletion

Import / Export in settings lets you export folders, tasks, images, and recently deleted content. Exported backup files are not encrypted by the app. Keep them secure; any storage or sharing provider you choose handles files under its own policy.

Deleted tasks move to Recently Deleted, where you can restore or permanently delete them. With sync enabled, deletion changes sync through iCloud. Uninstalling the app or turning off sync does not delete cloud data. Manage app data in Apple's iCloud storage settings and delete exported backups separately. Back up and turn sync off before switching Apple Accounts.

5. Optional tips, advertising, and diagnostics

The app has no third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs. We do not sell task content or use it for advertising profiles. Buy the developer a coffee is an optional, one-time Apple in-app purchase with no automatic renewal or additional task features. Apple processes payment; the app reads product information, purchase status, and transaction information for verification, not your full payment card details. See App Store & Privacy.

The app records error types and codes in device system logs for troubleshooting. It does not upload task content to a developer-operated analytics service. If you email us, we use the issue description, screenshots, system version, diagnostics, and contact information you choose to provide to respond and investigate. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive content.

6. This website, retention, and your choices

This website uses no analytics or advertising tracking scripts. Checklist demo interactions are not submitted or saved. Hosting and network providers may process IP addresses, request times, browser information, and access logs to deliver and secure the site. Your email provider handles messages under its own rules when you use an email link.

You control local content. iCloud data and backups are retained according to your chosen services and their rules. Support messages are kept only as needed to handle requests, provide follow-up support, or meet necessary obligations. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of support information we hold. We cannot read or directly manage your device or private iCloud data, but can explain the available controls.

We use safeguards appropriate to the information we handle, but no storage or communication method is completely secure. Processing locations and protections depend on the Apple, hosting, email, or other services you choose. The app is not directed specifically at children and does not intentionally build children's profiles. Parents or guardians can contact us about information sent to us voluntarily.

7. Updates and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date when features or information handling change.
